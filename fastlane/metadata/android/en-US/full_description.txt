Polyglot Books is the ultimate privacy-first audiobook player and ebook reader for book lovers, language learners, and avid readers worldwide.

Experience books in any language without language barriers using your own Google Gemini API key (Bring Your Own Key - BYOK). Polyglot Books connects directly from your device to Google Gemini, with zero middleman servers, zero advertising, and zero user tracking.

KEY FEATURES:

🎧 LIVE AUDIOBOOK TRANSLATION
- Listen to foreign-language audiobooks translated on the fly into 70+ languages.
- Real-time streaming powered by Google's Gemini Live Translation API.
- Intelligent look-ahead buffering ensures uninterrupted, buffer-free listening.
- Smart local caching: replaying or scrubbing back costs zero extra API tokens.
- Dual live subtitles: view original speech transcripts alongside real-time translated text.
- Audio ducking: choose between hearing pure translated audio or softly hearing the original narrator underneath at 15% volume.

📖 CHARACTER-CAST EBOOK READER
- Read your favorite books in EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, FB2, and text formats.
- Beautiful, responsive reading engine powered by foliate-js.
- Automated AI character casting: our intelligent analyzer detects characters, gender, and emotion per chapter to assign distinct, fitting voices to each persona.
- Pluggable speech synthesis: choose between ultra-natural Gemini Flash TTS, Gemini Live speech, or offline Android System TTS.
- Synchronized reading: karaoke-style sentence highlighting moves with the narration.

🔒 100% PRIVACY & BRING YOUR OWN KEY (BYOK)
- No subscriptions or paywalls. Use your personal free or paid Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
- Secure on-device encryption: your API key is encrypted using Google Tink AEAD and the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
- Complete data isolation: no third-party analytics SDKs, no telemetry, and 100% F-Droid compatible open-source architecture.

SUPPORTED FORMATS:
- Audiobooks: M4B, M4A, AAC, MP3, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, WAV
- Ebooks: EPUB (2 & 3), MOBI, AZW3 (KF8), FB2, CBZ, PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, RTF
